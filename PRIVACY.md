# Clockvoyant – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 27 September 2026_

Clockvoyant is a party timing game. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the game.

## What we collect

**Online games only.** When you create or join an online game, the app sends to our game server (Google Firebase Realtime Database):

- the **display name** you type (any nickname — it does not have to be your real name),
- the **game state** (the 4-letter game code, whose turn it is, round results).

This is visible to the other players in the same game and is used only to run that game. Games are **deleted automatically** after about 6 hours, or when every player has left.

**On your phone only.** Your sound volume, your last-used display name and your game stats (games played, wins) are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded.

## What we don't do

- No accounts, no sign-in, no email or phone number.
- No advertising, no ad IDs, no analytics or tracking.
- No location, contacts, camera, microphone or files.
- We never sell or share your data with anyone.

## Security

Data sent to the game server is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).

## Children

Clockvoyant is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

## Deleting your data

Online game data deletes itself automatically (see above). Local data is removed when you uninstall the app.
